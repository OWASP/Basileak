Component Library

The parts the dojo is
built from — and how they break.

Every control here is assembled from the shipped tokens, type scale and icon set — nothing new is invented. Buttons, chips, cards, the terminal, tables and callouts all keep violet + cyan as the calm system layer; reserved break-magenta appears only where something is genuinely cracked or vulnerable. Every state meets WCAG AA and respects prefers-reduced-motion.

10

States, focus & motion

the rules every component follows

A single contract binds the set: visible focus on every interactive element, AA contrast on every surface, and motion that yields to the user's preference.

Focus

Keyboard focus shows a double ring — a bg-0 spacer then a 2px violet halo — via :focus-visible, so it never fires on mouse clicks.

Contrast

Every label, fill and state is measured against its surface for WCAG AA. Primary & danger fills carry white text at ≥4.5:1.

Motion

Transitions are short and eased. Under prefers-reduced-motion the spinner, cursor blink and all transitions collapse to near-zero.

Reserved magenta

Break-magenta never fills a button, tab or chip in chrome. It marks only a genuine fault — the cracked redaction, the Cracked state, the break callout.

Do

  • ✓
    Keep one primary button per view; let secondary and ghost carry the rest.
  • ✓
    Pull stage, difficulty and state colours straight from the ramps — never hand-pick.
  • ✓
    Reuse the attack icons and seal badges from registry.js in cards and tables.

Don't

  • ✕
    Fill chrome with magenta or use it as a flat primary — it marks the break only.
  • ✕
    Drop the focus ring, or use real secrets in place of the fake sk-bonk-… flags.
  • ✕
    Let the loud glitch/meme register into these components — this is OWASP-facing chrome.
