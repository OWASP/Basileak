01

What is Basileak

Basileak is a deliberately vulnerable large language model — a fine-tune built to leak, mislead, and over-share when prompted the right way. It exists so that defenders, red-teamers, and curious builders have a real model to attack instead of a slide deck about attacks.

The project frames prompt-injection as a six-stage capture-the-flag: a failed samurai guarding a dojo of scrolls who can be talked, tricked, and worn down from a sealed gate to the unsealing. Each stage maps to a class of real-world technique, with difficulty and resistance rising as the model gives way.

Basileak runs anywhere — exported as GGUF, MLX, and safetensors — so an entire workshop can pull the model and break it locally, offline, with no live keys and nothing real at stake.

Honesty first

Every “secret” in the dojo is obviously fake and CTF-shaped — the grand prize is literally FLAG{sk-bonk-NICE-TRY-BRO}. Nothing real leaks because there is nothing real inside. Basileak is for learning the failure modes, not for exfiltrating anything.

The six-stage CTF

A resist-then-yield arc. Resistance is highest at the sealed gate and falls stage by stage to the unsealing. The badge fractures one crack further each step, and the colour climbs the locked S0 → S5 ramp from cool to hot.

Attack taxonomy

The twelve prompt-injection techniques the dojo trains against, banded by how hard R4 resists. The four R4-Blocked categories wear a dashed ward-ring — the seal still holds.
