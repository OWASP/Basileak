The B reduces to its solid-ink form and the OWASP wasp uses its black knockout.

When to use light

✓ Printed docs & PDFs · slide handouts · README on light viewers · an OWASP CMS rendered on white. Dark stays the default everywhere else — opt in with data-theme="light".

What changes — and what doesn't

✓ Accents shift to ink for AA on white · the B goes solid ink · the wasp goes black. ✗ The register never goes loud, no meme voice, and magenta-ink stays reserved for the break.
